### Introduction
Welcome to the inaugural edition of Sabha MUN at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras. 

Sabha is more than just a simulation; it is a premier forum designed to unite the sharpest minds across the country to negotiate, debate, and resolve complex global crises. Set against the backdrop of one of India’s most prestigious institutions, Sabha brings together 200+ elite delegates across five uniquely crafted committees.

From our flagship Crisis Committee designed for tech-forward minds, to the strategic budget wars of the PM Council, to the geopolitical trade strategies of the WTO—Sabha offers a rigorous platform where every lens is examined and real-world consensus is forged.

### The Committees

**1. UNGA — United Nations General Assembly**
Step into the theater of real-world impact. In the UNGA, negotiation meets necessity. Delegates will be challenged to transform the friction of competing national agendas into singular, universal consensus. 

**2. Crisis Committee — AI, Surveillance & Privacy**
*Flagship Committee*
Events unfold in real-time. Delegates must navigate the complex interaction between artificial intelligence, global surveillance, and personal privacy. Through secret directives, continuous intelligence leaks, and fast-evolving situations, every decision carries the weight to shift the balance between technological progress and ethical boundaries.

**3. WTO CTD — Committee on Trade and Development**
A test of economic resilience. The CTD tackles the imminent 2026 “Subsidy Race” and the fallout of global trade wars. Delegates are tasked with drafting a "Framework for Economic Resilience" that protects emerging markets from the decoupling strategies of economic superpowers.

**4. PM Council — Prime Minister's Council**
A high-stakes executive simulation. 30 Cabinet Ministers must navigate intense budget wars and departmental friction to reach a mandatory consensus. The objective is to draft a technically airtight Cabinet Note that delicately balances national survival with cold, hard fiscal reality.

**5. International Press**
Step behind the lens of raw reality. Members of the IP serve on the frontline of visual and narrative truth, wielding the power of journalism to expose the unseen and transform frozen moments into catalysts for real-world impact. Reporters, photographers, and caricaturists welcome.
